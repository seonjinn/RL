Evaluation Methodology

  • Matched comparisons keep source cohort, model, mode, max OSL, temperature/top_p, CUDA Graph state, cluster, node/GPU shape, attention/MoE backend, target TP, batch-token budget, segment, and recipe cohort fixed.
  • SpecDec rows add only the generation speculative decoding method, drafter/checkpoint, and num_speculative_tokens; baseline rows use the same recipe with SpecDec disabled.
  • Completed 20-step charts require either all 20 parsed metrics or the steady-state steps 2-20 with step 1 excluded. Partial step20 jobs are table-only.
