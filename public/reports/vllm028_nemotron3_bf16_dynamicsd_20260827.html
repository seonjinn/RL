What K means for these checkpoints

Both BF16 checkpoints declare num_nextn_predict_layers=1. K=2–5 therefore reuse the same native one-layer MTP block for multiple forwards; they do not select 2–5 distinct MTP heads. vLLM accepts positive K values divisible by the native depth. K1–K5 is the chosen practical sweep, not every theoretically accepted positive integer.
