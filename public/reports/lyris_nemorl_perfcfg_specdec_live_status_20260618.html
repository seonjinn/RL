Evaluation Methodology

  • Recipes: NeMo-RL examples/configs/recipes/llm/performance.
  • Sampling: temperature=1.0, top_p=1.0.
  • Max OSL: Qwen3-30B/32B use 4096; Qwen3-235B uses 8192.
  • Lyris GB200: 4 GPUs/node, no --gres, segment-aware submission.
  • Metrics: E2E throughput speedup, generation throughput speedup, E2E step time, acceptance rate, and mean accepted length.
