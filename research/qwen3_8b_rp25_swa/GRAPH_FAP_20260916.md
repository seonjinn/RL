## Why repeat the gate

The completed jobs7181268–7181270 used PIECEWISE + eager compilation backend.
Target capture finished, but that does not prove draft capture. In the pinned
vLLM v0.25.1 source, DFlashSpeculator.init_cudagraph_manager selects NONE unless
the requested decode mode is FULL; DSpark inherits this method. Its capture
announcement is printed even if no full graph is created.

Source: `vllm/v1/worker/gpu/spec_decode/dflash/speculator.py`, v0.25.1,
`init_cudagraph_manager` and `capture`; DSpark subclasses DFlashSpeculator in
`vllm/v1/worker/gpu/spec_decode/dspark/speculator.py`.

## Controlled rerun

Keep target, New Draft rp25-44000 B8, K5, DAPOMath17K, seed42, GBS512=64×8,
total32768/output30720, TP2 training/TP1 generation, four GB200 GPUs, three policy
steps, BF16, frozen drafters and pinned Aug18 image/vLLM0.25.1. No checkpoint
resume or online-cadence changes. No runtime patches to model computations.

Opt-in graph phases use mode3, backend=inductor, FULL_AND_PIECEWISE,
max_num_batched_tokens16384 and capture token buckets through16384. Small
buckets cover every request count at query lengths1,5,6 for S8/S32/S64; larger
mixed-prefill buckets are256,512,1024,2048,4096,8192,16384. These are scheduled
query-token counts, not context lengths. Capturing more shapes costs memory;
this gate may expose a capacity issue and is not a promise of OOM-free operation.

First submissions: Baseline default, Baseline S8, DFlash K5 S8, DSpark K5 S8.
No dependencies. This tests the FAP+Inductor optimization package; it does NOT
separate Inductor gains from graph-mode gains. S32/S64 configs are prepared but
held until target and draft FULL capture/replay, refit and quality gates pass.
No claim of an optimal concurrency or 200/1000-step online validation follows.

## Evidence and fail-closed checks

The opt-in `NRL_Q8_CG_AUDIT=1` branch in the NeMo-RL worker extension installs
CPU-only wrappers around MRv2 CudaGraphManager.capture/dispatch/run_fullgraph.
Normal runs are unchanged. No method output, graph descriptor or GPU kernel is
modified. The diagnostic refuses a different vLLM version/signature and raises
if a manager finishes capture with zero full graphs.

`Q8_CG_AUDIT` JSON records manager class, process, graph mode, full graph count,
successful FULL replay counts, dispatch counts by mode, uniform-decode fallback
count, and largest input/padded token counts. Records occur on capture, first
replay and every2048 dispatches/replays. These are cumulative snapshots, not
disjoint intervals: do not sum them. ModelCudaGraphManager is the target;
DFlashCudaGraphManager is the drafter for both DFlash and DSpark.

Counters are not GPU-time-weighted coverage; PIECEWISE dispatch alone is not
proof that every operation replayed a graph. Context-KV preprocessing and other
non-forward operations can remain eager by design. Native cudagraph_metrics is
also enabled, but the experiment audit is needed for MRv2/draft visibility.
Missing audit events or absent target/draft FULL replay must be reported as
unverified, not accepted as success. Inspect output samples, reward, entropy,
KL, lengths and preemption before expanding.

Stable study.py and matrix.py defaults remain unchanged. Old artifact paths and
W&B runs are preserved. New group: `q8-gbs512-32k-fap-coverage-20260916`.
Submission receipts and GPU results are pending.
