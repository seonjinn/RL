Root cause
----------

The dropless variable-size MCore AllToAll dispatcher copies split metadata to
the host and synchronizes a CUDA event before EP AllToAll. Host synchronization
is illegal inside CUDA Graph capture. Enabling A2A overlap does not remove this
dispatcher synchronization.

Corrective hypothesis
---------------------

Use the NeMo 26.06 GB200 performance dependency bundle: flex/HybridEP,
a positive static expert-rank token budget, PagedStash overflow retry,
Transformer Engine op fuser, GLU interleave 32, HybridEP preprocessing SMs,
and no expert activation offload. The corrected functional gate must prove one
capture and at least two replays before any performance cohort is accepted.

Performance claim boundary
--------------------------

Neither job produced a valid Full-CG or A2A-overlap performance result. The
12-cell timing cohort was test-only and was not submitted.
